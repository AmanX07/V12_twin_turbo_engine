How the V12 Twin-Turbo Ignites & Runs

1. Starter Cranking (280 RPM): Rear high-torque starter engages the flywheel ring gear, turning the 6-throw crankshaft while direct injectors prime the cylinders.

2. Sequential Spark Ignition: Coils fire every 60° of crank rotation (1-7-5-11-3-9-6-12-2-8-4-10), producing a balanced cold-start flare to 2,600 RPM before settling to a 1,100 RPM idle.

3. Twin-Turbo Spool (+1.65 Bar): High-enthalpy exhaust gases spin both front-mounted turbines up to 145,000 RPM, compressing intake air through the top charge pipes into the V12 plenums.
